## The panel

The Hadrian addon mirrors the theme's aesthetic inside WHMCS admin: a fixed-width, centered layout with a pill navigation and iOS-style controls. Changes preview live and apply instantly.

:::shot Hadrian admin panel

## Sections

- **Styles** - pick a preset, then edit colors, type, and components.
- **Layouts** - choose navigation and page structures.
- **Menu** - build client and guest menus per location.
- **Pages** - per-page template, SEO, and visibility.
- **Settings** - global behaviour, including the dark-mode controls under Appearance.
- **Branding** - logo, favicon and the media library.
- **Sitemap** - the generated sitemap and its inclusion rules.
- **Extensions** - the companion modules detected on this install.
- **Tools** - maintenance actions such as cache clearing.
- **Info** - version, environment and your license key.
