FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does Hadrian keep working if I don't renew updates?

Yes. The license is a one-time purchase. If you choose not to renew annual update access you lose new releases and support, but the theme keeps working on your installation.

What are the requirements?

WHMCS 9.0 or later, PHP 8.1 or later, and the ionCube loader. Hadrian targets Smarty 4 and falls back to Smarty 3 automatically.

What is the difference between Single Site and Unlimited?

Single Site covers one WHMCS installation. Unlimited covers every installation you run, and includes priority support. Both are one-time purchases with a year of updates included.

Do you offer a trial?

No trial licenses — instead, the live demo lets you click through every screen, and every purchase has a 14-day refund window so you can evaluate the theme on your own installation.

Can I change colours beyond the nine presets?

Yes. A preset is a starting point: behind it sit 57 colour tokens in eight groups, each editable in the admin, with separate light and dark values. Overrides are stored as diffs so any token can be reset to its default.

Do I need to write code to change the menus?

No. The Menu Manager lives in the admin: ten item types, drag to nest and reorder, labels per language or bound to WHMCS language variables, and per-layout visibility — no hooks, no template edits.
