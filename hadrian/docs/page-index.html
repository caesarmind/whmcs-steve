Client-area routes come from hadrian/scripts/pages.csv (the route map used by scripts/check-pages.sh); build-status tags (full / scaffold) come from hadrian/docs/PAGE-CHECKLIST.md and are a hint only. The Order form (cart) section lists every standalone hadrian_cart template (which inherits standard_cart): entry = directly reachable, mid-flow = needs items in the cart, conditional = only renders under specific conditions (fraud, error, social-login) so it has no bookmarkable URL — verify those in-flow.

Most client-area pages require a logged-in session — sign in to bill.hostnodes.com first, then the links resolve. Detail pages use placeholder IDs (id=1); change the ID in the URL if needed. Cart links carry ?carttpl=hadrian_cart to force the order-form theme, and mid-flow cart pages (view cart, checkout, complete) may redirect unless you have items in the cart.
